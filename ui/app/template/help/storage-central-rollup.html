Response time and JVM gauge data — Cassandra TTL in days for chart aggregates sent by agents.
  • 1 min → 4 hour — how long to retain each rollup resolution. Finer intervals need more storage for the same history.
  • 1-minute retention also covers the finer gauge samples agents collect.
TTL applies to new writes. Existing cells keep the TTL they were written with. See the wiki for TWCS notes.
