Chart navigation tips
  • drag or double click to zoom in
  • use ctrl + scroll wheel to zoom in and out
Sampling
  • gauge values are collected every 5 seconds by default
  • brief spikes between samples (or flattened by rollup averages on longer ranges) may not appear
  • for denser sampling in a test environment only, use -Dglowroot.internal.gaugeCollectionIntervalMillis=1000 (unsupported for production; more overhead)
